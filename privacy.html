Privacy information

Prototype notice: This page describes the current portfolio prototype, not a live financial service privacy policy. A production service requires a jurisdiction-specific policy reviewed by qualified legal and privacy professionals.

What this prototype does

The Rogontra Pay prototype is a static front-end demonstration. Its authentication, transfer, dashboard, and account screens do not connect to a server or financial institution.

Information entered into forms

The sign-in, sign-up, and transfer forms use browser-side validation to demonstrate the intended interface. They do not submit, retain, or create real customer records. The contact form is also not connected to a support system in this prototype.

Future production requirements

Before any live launch, Rogontra Pay would need a defined data inventory, lawful basis for processing, retention schedule, secure infrastructure, clear consent and rights processes, incident response plan, and a full privacy notice tailored to the locations in which it operates.
