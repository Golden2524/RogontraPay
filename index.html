Questions, answered

Start with the essentials.

Can I make a real payment with this site?

No. This is a portfolio prototype. Buttons and forms demonstrate the intended product experience but do not create accounts, store credentials, or process payments.

Where can I find product and safety information?

The Trust Center explains the prototype’s boundaries and links to its privacy, terms, and help pages.

What will happen when the backend is added?

Real authentication, verification, payment processing, account data, and support workflows will only be enabled after secure backend implementation and appropriate legal review.
