About the project

A clearer way to understand everyday money.

Rogontra Pay is a frontend digital-wallet concept that explores calm, accessible interfaces for balances, transfers, and transaction history.

Design principles

Built to keep the important details in view.

01

Clarity before action

Important information is grouped and written plainly, helping users understand the next step before they take it.

02

Review before progress

The transfer flow creates a deliberate review step so recipients, amounts, and narrations can be checked before a future confirmation stage.

03

Honest product boundaries

This public version is a frontend prototype. It does not process payments, create accounts, or store personal information.
