Help Center

How can we help?

Answers about the current Rogontra Pay prototype and its planned product experience.

Is Rogontra Pay live?

Not yet. This public site is a front-end portfolio prototype. It does not handle real financial activity.

Why can’t I log in or create an account?

Authentication is currently visual only. Secure account creation requires a backend, identity verification flow, and appropriate data protection controls.

Will the transfer button send money?

No. The transfer screens validate sample inputs and show a review step only. No bank or wallet connections are present.

How will support work later?

A live version should offer a verified support channel, case tracking, clear response times, and an escalation route for security concerns.
